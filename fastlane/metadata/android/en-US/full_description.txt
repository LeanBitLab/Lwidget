Lwidget is a modern, ultra-lightweight, open-source Android widget built with Kotlin and Material 3 design principles. It provides essential information on your home screen at a glance while adhering to your device's dynamic theme.

★ Key Features:
• Material You: Full Android dynamic color support with seamless light and dark mode adaptation.
• Time & Date: Clear, customizable display with 12h/24h formats and customizable date formats.
• Battery & Temperature: Real-time battery percentage and device temperature status.
• Step Counter: Optional daily step counter with a lightweight foreground service.
• Calendar Events: Upcoming agenda at a glance with timezone-aware all-day event handling.
• Tasks Integration: Seamless integration with Tasks.org.
• Weather: Current conditions and weekly forecast warnings via Breezy Weather integration.
• World Clock: Track time in another timezone.
• Next Alarm: Display your upcoming scheduled alarm.
• Daily Data Usage: Monitor your cellular data consumption directly on your home screen.
• Screen Time: Track your daily device usage with zero wake locks.
• System Stats: Monitor internal storage and RAM availability.
• Drag-and-Drop Reordering: Rearrange right-side widget statistics in any order.
• Typography: 11 distinct font styles (Default, Serif, Monospace, Cursive, Condensed, Light, Medium, Black, Thin, Small Caps).
• Outline & Transparency: Fine-tune widget opacity, custom colors, and accent outlines.
• Custom App Launchers: Choose your preferred Clock and Calendar apps to launch when tapping time and date.

★ 100% Privacy Focused:
• No internet permission required: Lwidget is strictly offline and cannot connect to any remote server.
• Zero analytics, zero trackers, zero advertisements.
• GPLv3 Free and Open Source Software (FOSS).
