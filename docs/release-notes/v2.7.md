## 🚀 What's New in v2.7

### 🧭 Rebuilt Settings Navigation & Auto-Hiding Top Bar
* **Slim Material 3 Header**: Rebuilt the top navigation into a slim 56dp TopAppBar featuring direct widget switching on the left and an expandable search icon button on the right.
* **Auto-Hiding Scroll**: The header and search bar seamlessly auto-hide on scroll down and re-appear on scroll up with snap animations, giving full focus to settings.
* **Status Bar Protection**: Applied system window insets and surface scrimming to prevent scrolling cards from bleeding under the transparent status bar icons.
* **Persistent Preview**: The live widget preview remains pinned cleanly above the category selector tabs.

### 👟 Pedometer Stability & Decoupled Notifications
* **Reboot Resilience**: Fixed pedometer reset issues across device reboots by dynamically recalculating baseline step deltas.
* **Decoupled Notification Permission**: On Android 13+, denying notification permission no longer disables the step counter - the foreground service runs reliably in the background without cluttering your notification shade.
* **Live Step Notification**: The ongoing service notification now dynamically displays your real-time daily step count (e.g., "4,520 steps today") alongside widget updates.
* **Independent Keep Alive**: Keep Alive now runs without requiring physical activity sensor permissions.

### ✨ Visual Polish & Unified Material Design
* **Ambient Text Shadows**: Added subtle, high-legibility text shadows across all 11 font layout styles and event displays for crisp contrast on bright wallpapers.
* **Inline Battery Charging Indicator**: Displays a clean inline lightning glyph (`⚡78%`) with proportional sizing when your device is charging.
* **Refined Alarm Indicator**: Updated the alarm separator dot (`•`) for improved visual balance across all layouts.
* **Icon System Overhaul**: Modernized all 39 vector icons across the app and widget to clean, consistent, pixel-perfect Material 3 vectors.

### ⚡ Battery & Performance Enhancements
* **Pocket Mode Optimization**: Suppresses widget updates while the screen is off and immediately synchronizes upon waking your device.
* **Intelligent Walk Throttling**: Throttles active walking updates to once every 10 seconds or 15 steps with guaranteed trailing-edge synchronization.
* **Broadcast Deduplication**: Eliminates redundant widget updates from minor microvolt battery fluctuation broadcasts.
* **Binder IPC Reduction**: Cached package launch intent lookups, eliminating repeated system service IPC round-trips.

### 🌐 100% Complete Translations
* **All Languages Synchronized**: Filled all missing translations across all 10 non-English languages (Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Japanese, Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, and Chinese) for complete multi-language coverage.
